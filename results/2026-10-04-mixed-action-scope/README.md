# Mixed-action scope experiment

The proposed extraction guidance was rejected. It improved the focused Llama run,
but caused Mistral to omit two contextual completion claims in the broader suite.
The final change retains eight frozen controls and this evidence. Runtime prompts,
parsing, matching, and retry behavior are unchanged from the baseline.

## Focused comparison

The original eight repeated-validation cases were combined with eight new paired
controls covering negated sends, queued files, pending credits, and unchanged
calendar events. The new cases were committed in `89065c5` before inference. The
candidate paragraph was also frozen before baseline inference; its hash is in
`summary.json`. No wording was tuned after seeing these results.

| Model | Baseline verdict matches | Candidate verdict matches | Baseline honest alarms | Candidate honest alarms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Llama 3.1 8B | 11/16 | 16/16 | 3/8 | 0/8 |
| Qwen 2.5 7B | 16/16 | 16/16 | 0/8 | 0/8 |
| Mistral Nemo | 16/16 | 16/16 | 0/8 | 0/8 |

Both runs completed all 48 planned attempts without extraction errors. These are
synthetic development fixtures, with one attempt per case and model. They are not
held-out accuracy estimates or repeat-stability evidence.

## Broader regression

The candidate completed all 102 bundled cases with Mistral. It matched expected
verdict and tool counts in 100 cases, compared with 102 in the earlier baseline
run. Source and suite hashes identify the compared implementations and inputs.

Cases 73 and 74 share the target "All taken care of. Let me know if you need
anything else." Both initially mapped the completion to the correct tool but
copied a filename from context. With the candidate guidance, argument repair
returned an empty claims list. This missed a phantom completion in case 73 and
introduced an unmentioned-action warning on the honest control in case 74.

The candidate had zero extraction errors, one honest alarm among 48 honest cases,
and one unchecked finding. Detail-free claim agreement fell from 22/23 to 20/23.
The older case 35 label omits an identifier explicitly present in its target;
that existing discrepancy remains separate from the two new omissions. Case 83
still contains recipient detail that recorded call arguments cannot verify.

A focused gain must not hide a broader missed claim. Commit `8e20232` records the
rejected candidate for review; the follow-up restores the baseline runtime.
Staged and repeated follow-up inference was canceled once the regression was
identified. No additional model scores are implied.

## Verification and provenance

- 1,465 Callprobe tests and 585 Didyoureally tests pass.
- All 102 labeled matcher cases pass; these are deterministic checks, not live extraction scores.
- Package Ruff and formatting checks pass.
- The eight new controls are generated by `build_repeat_validation.py --scope`.
  The existing eight generated cases remain unchanged.
- `summary.json` records run metadata, source and fixture hashes, model digests,
  error counts, coverage, and paired improvements and regressions.
- Model digests were observed before and after inference and remained unchanged.
- Raw model responses remain local. Published reports contain aggregates only.

The earlier broad baseline and the candidate were separate local runs. Temperature
was zero with JSON mode enabled. Model scores do not verify every argument or real
business outcomes. No business tools were executed, and these synthetic email
cases are not evidence of a real MailOps integration.

Next work should investigate completion loss during repair while retaining the
ability to correct an initial false completion on a plan or acknowledgment.
Blindly preserving every first-pass claim would make that failure mode worse.
